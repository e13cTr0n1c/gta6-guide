Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

Not at launch. Rockstar calls GTA 6 a single-player experience and has not announced an online mode or a date, though existing GTA Online carries on.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

What was said about launch

GTA VI launched as a 'single-player experience' (Rockstar to IGN and storefronts). No new online mode at launch; Rockstar has not announced a GTA VI Online name or date.

  • IGN (Africa edition, Wesley Yin-Poole) (24 June 2026)

Take-Two's Strauss Zelnick has said Take-Two supports legacy titles while communities stay engaged (NBA 2K Online example), suggesting existing GTA Online continues.

  • IGN (Africa edition, Wesley Yin-Poole) (24 June 2026)

What is only speculation

I am labelling this as speculation so it does not read like a date.

GameSpot notes reports that the story appears as a separate download, fuelling speculation an online mode is not far off. Speculation only.

This is speculation. I am not treating it as a feature that exists.
