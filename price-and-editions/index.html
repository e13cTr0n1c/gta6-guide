How much is GTA 6 in the UK? Standard vs Ultimate

Standard costs £69.99 and Ultimate costs £89.99 (US $79.99 and $99.99). Ultimate is digital and adds the '95 Grotti Cheetah, extra revolvers, a garage and a buggy, and five extra shops. Boxed copies contain a download code, not a disc.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

The sticker prices

I do not have a confirmed UK price for a standalone Ultimate upgrade. The fact file says that part is still unknown.

UK price: Standard Edition £69.99, Ultimate Edition £89.99. US: $79.99 / $99.99.

  • PC Gamer (25 June 2026)

Eurozone price: Standard €79.99, Ultimate €99.99.

  • Eurogamer.de (25 June 2026)

Ultimate Edition upgrade: PC Gamer describes it as a '$20 upgrade'; the exact UK price of a standalone upgrade was not found in the reputable sources checked.

Part of this is still unknown in the sources I checked.

Note from the fact file: Non-list sources (The Scotsman, GTALAB) say the upgrade is £20 / buyable later; not recorded as confirmed.

  • PC Gamer (25 June 2026)

What Ultimate includes

The contents below are the list stored in the fact file. I have not shortened the names.

Ultimate Edition contents (Rockstar text via PC Gamer): '95 Grotti Cheetah; Ganado Retro Build; Shitzu Squalo boat; '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Paradise Garage (Watson Bay); Jason's Safehouse Vehicles (Dinka Enduro motorcycle, Crest Kayak); Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers; personalised Girardi ES9 and Klose K17 pistols; Vice City Style; Goodtime Gear; Classic Car Collection activity (Wyman); PTT Youngin$ Compound; Rideout Customs; Sara's Unisex Salon; Stock 305; Electric Fang Tattoo; One-Eyed Willie's.

  • PC Gamer (25 June 2026)

Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack ('55 Vapid Stanier stored at Shore Court garage, outfits/hairstyles, tropical weapon pattern) plus one month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders.

  • PC Gamer (25 June 2026)
  • IGN (Africa edition, Wesley Yin-Poole) (24 June 2026)

On PlayStation the Ultimate Edition is sold via PlayStation Store only; upgrades from digital Standard or code-in-box Standard are only on PlayStation Store.

  • PlayStation UK support (official) (undated)

Boxed copies

Physical (code-in-box) copies contain a download code, not a disc. Code-in-box versions 'should' be at retailers from 12 November.
