How do you buy a car in GTA 6?

I have not found a dealer in the guides so far. I steal a car, then pay to register it: with Moses from Chapter 2, at Pay 'n' Spray from Chapter 3, and high-end cars with Randy. The WAiNK app shows each car's registration cost before I take it.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

How I take ownership

I am careful with the no dealer line, because the fact file says that part is inferred from silence.

There are no car dealerships to buy from in the reputable guides checked: you take ownership by stealing a car and registering it (fee shown in the WAiNK app).

The registration mechanic is confirmed. The line that there are no dealers is inferred from the guides staying silent, so I am treating that part carefully.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

Registration unlock order (Polygon): Ch 2 Moses registers untracked cars; Ch 3 any Pay 'N' Spray registers cars without advanced trackers; Randy (Ch 3) registers high-end cars even with trackers. Fee: a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Parking is not the same as owning

A garage can keep a stolen car safe. That still does not make it mine.

Parking a stolen car in a garage keeps it safe but does not give ownership; leaving it outside means losing it.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

You can't own extra personal vehicles until around the 30% story mark (Port Gellhorn/Moses). You start with four owned vehicle slots; buying garages adds slots; Valentina (mid-story) gives an Ocean Beach garage with two slots; 12+ slots possible.

  • GameSpot (18 November 2026)

Scooter Bros app delivers owned vehicles for a fee (more for distance/damage) and recovers impounded or destroyed personal vehicles; unlocks after Moses' quest in Ch 2.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Where a sale goes

Pay 'n' Spray locations unlock during the Chapter 1 mission 'Just a Scratch'; they repair, respray (lowers vehicle heat) and buy cars. Sale money goes into BuckMe.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)
