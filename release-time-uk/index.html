When did GTA 6 come out in the UK?

It has already happened. GTA 6 unlocked at midnight GMT on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PS5 copies unlocked by store region, and Xbox players could start earlier by changing their time zone. I am writing this on 20 November 2026, so this is a record of the launch.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

What had already happened by the next day

Read this as history. The UK unlock was midnight GMT on 19 November 2026, and I checked the record on 20 November 2026.

GTA VI is out now worldwide on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S; Rockstar's 'Out Now' post is dated 19 November 2026.

  • Rockstar Games (official) (19 November 2026)

UK release: 00:00 local time (GMT) on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PS5. The UK is on GMT in November, so midnight UK time.

Note from the fact file: The premise that it 'has not launched in the UK yet' is outdated: by 20 Nov 2026 it has been out in the UK for over a day.

How the rolling unlock worked

One of these points is only reported, and the note on US time zones is unclear in the source, so I am not tidying it up.

Release used a rolling local-midnight unlock on 19 November 2026 in each region, starting at 00:00 NZDT in New Zealand (11:00 GMT on 18 Nov).

  • PlayStation Blog (official) (10 November 2026)
  • GamesRadar+ (pre-launch, Nov 2026 (exact date not shown))

On PS5 the unlock follows the PlayStation Store region of purchase; on Xbox (single global store) GamesRadar reports players could get earlier access by changing the console time zone (the 'New Zealand' method).

  • GamesRadar+ (pre-launch, Nov 2026 (exact date not shown))

GamesRadar's own testing showed the US PSN Store set to midnight EST (9pm PST on 18 Nov); unclear whether other US time zones were corrected.

This is reported. I have not treated it as confirmed.
