Buying and the launch

  • When did GTA 6 come out in the UK?It has already happened. GTA 6 unlocked at midnight GMT on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PS5 copies unlocked by store region, and Xbox players could start earlier by changing their time zone. I am writing this on 20 November 2026, so this is a record of the launch.
  • How much is GTA 6 in the UK? Standard vs UltimateStandard costs £69.99 and Ultimate costs £89.99 (US $79.99 and $99.99). Ultimate is digital and adds the '95 Grotti Cheetah, extra revolvers, a garage and a buggy, and five extra shops. Boxed copies contain a download code, not a disc.
  • How big is GTA 6? File size and storageRockstar says up to 212GB depending on the device, and the PlayStation page says to clear at least 212GB. Preload opened on 10 November. I would clear 212GB, because the fact file says that is the safe reading when the wording differs.
  • Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?Not at launch. Rockstar calls GTA 6 a single-player experience and has not announced an online mode or a date, though existing GTA Online carries on.
  • Is GTA 6 on PC?Not yet. It is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Xbox One can play through Xbox Cloud Gaming. A GamesBeat report says a PC release in Q1 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Money

  • How to make money fast in GTA 6Early on, I rob Illegal Operations and sell the drugs to my fence, and I sell stolen cars at Pay 'n' Spray or to Moses. From Chapter 3 the snap gun opens back-door robberies worth $6,000 to $15,000, and Pilot Pete's drops in Chapter 4 pay $10,000 to $20,000 a run.
  • How does the bank (BuckMe) and Family Savings work?Each character can carry up to $20,000 in cash, and I lose it if I am Wasted or Busted, so I bank it at an ATM. Purchases over $1,000 go through BuckMe checking. Money in Family Savings can never come back out, and it affects the ending.

Cars, garages and a home

  • How do you buy a car in GTA 6?I have not found a dealer in the guides so far. I steal a car, then pay to register it: with Moses from Chapter 2, at Pay 'n' Spray from Chapter 3, and high-end cars with Randy. The WAiNK app shows each car's registration cost before I take it.
  • How to steal cars in GTA 6 (slim jim, key cloner, trackers)I scan a car with the WAiNK app first. A slim jim from Moses in Chapter 2 opens locks quietly, and the key cloner from Chapter 3 is needed for keyless cars. Advanced trackers alert the police unless I keep driving fast.
  • All GTA 6 garage prices and locationsThere are 10 garages, from Mitchel Auto Center at $4,000 to Atlantic Court in Ocean Beach at $40,000, bought in the Vice City Real Estate app. Two are free: Port Gellhorn Station from Moses, and Paradise Garage with the Ultimate Edition.
  • Can you buy a house in GTA 6?No. I cannot buy my own home. The Vice City Real Estate app only sells garages, and safehouses come with the story.
  • Where to find a tank in GTA 6The only Hauners Rhino tanks found so far are at Mile Zero Air Station, west of Key Lento in the Leonida Keys, usually in the hangars. If I am spotted getting in, I get an instant five-star wanted level.

Playing

  • How to save in GTA 6I press Start or Options, choose Game, pick one of 15 slots and select Save Game. There is also one autosave slot, but I cannot save during a mission, and using a cheat stops saving for that session.
  • How to switch between Jason and LuciaFree switching unlocks after the Chapter 2 mission 'Breakfast Run'. After that, I hold Right on the D-pad and pick a character in the bottom right. The same menu can summon my partner or split us up.
  • How to lose your wanted level (heat) in GTA 6First I break line of sight and get out of the red heat zone on the map. Then I clear each heat icon: I change my outfit or put on a hat and sunglasses, swap or respray the car, stow longarms, split from my partner, and destroy CCTV.
  • GTA 6 cheats and how to unlock themCheats only unlock after I finish the story, and each one needs its own challenge. To enter one, I open the phone, hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB) and press the D-pad code. The $250,000 cheat is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right, and it disables saving.
  • Every GTA 6 phone app and what it doesI press Up on the D-pad for the phone. The key apps are BuckMe for the bank, RydeMe for a taxi and fast travel, Scooter Bros for car delivery, WAiNK for the car scanner, and Vice City Real Estate for garages.
  • How long is GTA 6 and how many missions?There are 91 main missions over five chapters. GameSpot puts the story at 65+ hours, 90 to 100 hours with side content, and 200+ hours for 100%.
