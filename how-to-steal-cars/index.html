How to steal cars in GTA 6 (slim jim, key cloner, trackers)

I scan a car with the WAiNK app first. A slim jim from Moses in Chapter 2 opens locks quietly, and the key cloner from Chapter 3 is needed for keyless cars. Advanced trackers alert the police unless I keep driving fast.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

The tools, in story order

Car-stealing tools: WAiNK app (Ch 1 mission 'Unwanted Attention') scans lock, alarm, keyless, trackers, value, registration cost; slim jim from Moses (Ch 2 mission 'Moses'); key cloner (Ch 3 mission 'Steal the Zorruso for Randy') opens and starts keyless cars. Advanced trackers alert police unless you keep driving fast.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)

Keeping the car afterwards

There are no car dealerships to buy from in the reputable guides checked: you take ownership by stealing a car and registering it (fee shown in the WAiNK app).

The registration mechanic is confirmed. The line that there are no dealers is inferred from the guides staying silent, so I am treating that part carefully.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

Registration unlock order (Polygon): Ch 2 Moses registers untracked cars; Ch 3 any Pay 'N' Spray registers cars without advanced trackers; Randy (Ch 3) registers high-end cars even with trackers. Fee: a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Parking a stolen car in a garage keeps it safe but does not give ownership; leaving it outside means losing it.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

Selling, and the limit the guides do not share

Stealing cars: Moses (Ch 2) buys older, low-profile cars, ~$1,000 to a few thousand each; Randy (Ch 3) pays more for high-end cars. Range quoted $1,000 to $20,000.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)

Pay 'n' Spray locations unlock during the Chapter 1 mission 'Just a Scratch'; they repair, respray (lowers vehicle heat) and buy cars. Sale money goes into BuckMe.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

Pay 'N' Spray car-sale limits disagree: PC Gamer says three cars per day; VICE says a limit per 24 in-game hours (and Moses has no daily limit); Polygon says a brief cooldown.

Sources differ on this, so I am showing the disagreement instead of picking a winner.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
