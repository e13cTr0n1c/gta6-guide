How to switch between Jason and Lucia

Free switching unlocks after the Chapter 2 mission 'Breakfast Run'. After that, I hold Right on the D-pad and pick a character in the bottom right. The same menu can summon my partner or split us up.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

Who the two characters are

Protagonists: Jason Duval (ex-Army, working for drug runners in the Keys) and Lucia Caminos (out of Leonida Penitentiary, family from Liberty City).

  • PlayStation Blog (official, Rockstar post) (18 November 2026)

When the switch unlocks

Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks after 'Breakfast Run' (Ch 2, mission 8), which may take 10+ hours. Hold Right on the D-pad and pick a profile (bottom right). Same menu can summon your partner or split up. Some missions are J-only or L-only.
