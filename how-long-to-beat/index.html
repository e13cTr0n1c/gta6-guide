How long is GTA 6 and how many missions?

There are 91 main missions over five chapters. GameSpot puts the story at 65+ hours, 90 to 100 hours with side content, and 200+ hours for 100%.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

Missions and hours

91 main missions across five chapters (22, 11, 26, 25, 7). Main story 65+ hours; GameSpot staff with side content 90 to 100 hours; 100% estimated 200+ hours.

  • GameSpot (18 November 2026)

How the opening feels

The opening is long, and free character switching is one of the things that waits.

Don't worry too much about money early: the opening chapters can take 20+ hours and money gets easier later.

  • The Guardian (Keza MacDonald) (19 November 2026)

Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks after 'Breakfast Run' (Ch 2, mission 8), which may take 10+ hours. Hold Right on the D-pad and pick a profile (bottom right). Same menu can summon your partner or split up. Some missions are J-only or L-only.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
