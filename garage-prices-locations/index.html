All GTA 6 garage prices and locations

There are 10 garages, from Mitchel Auto Center at $4,000 to Atlantic Court in Ocean Beach at $40,000, bought in the Vice City Real Estate app. Two are free: Port Gellhorn Station from Moses, and Paradise Garage with the Ultimate Edition.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

The ten published prices

These are the 10 garages in the fact file, in the stored order, with the dollar prices GameSpot published. I have not converted them to pounds.

GameSpot lists 10 garages; none in Lummox County. Other free garages from reputable/other coverage: Valentina's Ocean Beach garage (GameSpot, 2 slots); Shore Court garage with the pre-order '55 Vapid Stanier (PC Gamer). Non-list sources also mention a GTA+ Crosstown garage; not confirmed by a list outlet.

Garage prices and locations: see prices table (10 garages per GameSpot).

  • GameSpot (18 November 2026)

Vice City Real Estate unlocks after Moses' first quest in Ch 2, when you steal the '82 Crawler for him.

Note from the fact file: Non-list sources say after mission 'Daily Driver' (Ch 2, mission 6), which may be the same moment.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

How many vehicles fit

Sources differ on capacity, so I show both accounts and I do not invent a slot count for each address.

Garage capacity disagreement: PC Gamer says each garage holds a single car; GameSpot says garages hold 'one or a couple of vehicles'.

Sources differ on this, so I am showing the disagreement instead of picking a winner.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
  • GameSpot (18 November 2026)

You can't own extra personal vehicles until around the 30% story mark (Port Gellhorn/Moses). You start with four owned vehicle slots; buying garages adds slots; Valentina (mid-story) gives an Ocean Beach garage with two slots; 12+ slots possible.

  • GameSpot (18 November 2026)
