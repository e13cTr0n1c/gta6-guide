## Headline: release status (20 Nov 2026)

GTA VI is **already out in the UK**. It unlocked at 00:00 GMT on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, after a rolling local-midnight launch that began in New Zealand at 11:00 GMT on 18 Nov. Rockstar's 'Out Now' post (19 Nov) says it's available worldwide. The brief's premise (not yet out in the UK) is out of date. No PC version has been announced, and there's no online mode at launch.

## Saving

- **F051** [confirmed] Manual save: Start/Options > Game > choose slot > Save Game. 15 manual slots plus one autosave slot (after missions, Wasted/Busted, major events). Can't save during missions. No mission replay. No in-game quit option.  
  Sources: [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-how-to-save/) (2026-11-19 (last updated)). Checked 2026-11-20.
- **F052** [confirmed] Using any cheat disables saving for that session.  
  Sources: [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-how-to-save/) (2026-11-19 (last updated)); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/all-gta-6-cheats-and-how-to-unlock-them/) (2026-11-18). Checked 2026-11-20.

## Switching

- **F053** [confirmed] Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks after 'Breakfast Run' (Ch 2, mission 8), which may take 10+ hours. Hold Right on the D-pad and pick a profile (bottom right). Same menu can summon your partner or split up. Some missions are J-only or L-only.  
  Sources: [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-how-to-change-character-swap-jason-lucia-grand-theft-auto/) (2026-11-19); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-long-to-beat-gta-6/) (2026-11-18). Checked 2026-11-20.

## Wanted

- **F054** [confirmed] Stars show wanted level (up to six). 1 star ~ one police car, 2 stars several cars, 3 stars helicopter. Escape the red heat zone to clear stars.  
  Sources: [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-how-to-lower-grand-theft-auto/) (2026-11-19). Checked 2026-11-20.
- **F055** [confirmed] Heat icons and fixes: appearance (cap/sunglasses/haircut), outfit (change clothes), vehicle (respray at Pay 'N' Spray or swap), visible weapon (holster or stow longarm in trunk), partner (split up), CCTV (destroy recorder; cloud CCTV can't be destroyed).  
  Sources: [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-wanted-level-heat-how-to-lower-grand-theft-auto/) (2026-11-19). Checked 2026-11-20.
- **F056** [confirmed (single source)] Guardian: stealing a car is one star, robbing a store three or more, killing cops always adds a star.  
  Sources: [The Guardian (Keza MacDonald)](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/nov/19/tips-for-playing-grand-theft-auto-vi) (2026-11-19). Checked 2026-11-20.
