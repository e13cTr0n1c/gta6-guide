Where to find a tank in GTA 6

The only Hauners Rhino tanks found so far are at Mile Zero Air Station, west of Key Lento in the Leonida Keys, usually in the hangars. If I am spotted getting in, I get an instant five-star wanted level.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

The only place the guide names

Tank: the only Hauners Rhino tanks found are at Mile Zero Air Station, west of Key Lento in the Leonida Keys, usually in hangars on the north and south sides. Being spotted gives an instant five-star wanted level.

  • GameSpot (19 November 2026)

What the star scale is

The five-star warning sits on the normal star scale, which goes up to six.

Stars show wanted level (up to six). 1 star ~ one police car, 2 stars several cars, 3 stars helicopter. Escape the red heat zone to clear stars.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
