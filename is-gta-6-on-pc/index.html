Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not yet. It is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Xbox One can play through Xbox Cloud Gaming. A GamesBeat report says a PC release in Q1 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

Where I can play it now

Platforms at launch: PS5 (PS5 Pro enhanced) and Xbox Series X|S. Not playable on PS4.

  • PlayStation US store page (official) (undated)
  • Rockstar Games (official) (19 November 2026)

Xbox Cloud Gaming available at launch, which also lets Xbox One play via streaming. No cloud streaming on PlayStation; Remote Play from your own console supported.

  • GameSpot (10 November 2026)

GTA VI is out now worldwide on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S; Rockstar's 'Out Now' post is dated 19 November 2026.

  • Rockstar Games (official) (19 November 2026)

The PC report

This report is unconfirmed. I am not treating Q1 2027 as a release date.

PC: no PC version announced by Rockstar. GamesBeat (via Polygon) reported a PC release in Q1 2027 from four anonymous sources; unconfirmed.

This is reported and unconfirmed.
