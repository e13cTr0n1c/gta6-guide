Every GTA 6 phone app and what it does

I press Up on the D-pad for the phone. The key apps are BuckMe for the bank, RydeMe for a taxi and fast travel, Scooter Bros for car delivery, WAiNK for the car scanner, and Vice City Real Estate for garages.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

How I open it

Open the phone with D-pad Up; hold Up for WAiNK shortcut.
