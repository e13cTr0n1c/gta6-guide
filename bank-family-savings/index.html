How does the bank (BuckMe) and Family Savings work?

Each character can carry up to $20,000 in cash, and I lose it if I am Wasted or Busted, so I bank it at an ATM. Purchases over $1,000 go through BuckMe checking. Money in Family Savings can never come back out, and it affects the ending.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

Pocket cash

Pocket cash: each character can carry up to $20,000. All pocket cash is lost if Wasted or Busted. Cash covers purchases up to $1,000; larger purchases need BuckMe.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

Before ATMs, stash cash under Jason's bedroom floorboard; deposit at ATMs (find via BuckMe 'ATM Finder').

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

BuckMe checking

The guides say the balance limits rise later in the story. They do not state the dollar amount of each limit, so I am not filling those in.

BuckMe checking account unlocks in Chapter 1 (mission 'Buck Me'); each character has their own; balance limits rise with story progress (upgrades in Ch 2, 3 and 4). Over the limit, incoming payments are held as pending.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Family Savings

The guides name five tiers. They do not list a dollar amount for each tier in the fact I have.

Family Savings is a shared Jason/Lucia account. Money put in can never be withdrawn or spent. Five tiers. It affects some interactions and influences the ending.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
