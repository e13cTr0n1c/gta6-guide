How to make money fast in GTA 6

Early on, I rob Illegal Operations and sell the drugs to my fence, and I sell stolen cars at Pay 'n' Spray or to Moses. From Chapter 3 the snap gun opens back-door robberies worth $6,000 to $15,000, and Pilot Pete's drops in Chapter 4 pay $10,000 to $20,000 a run.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

What I do in the opening chapters

I stick to the jobs the guides name for the early chapters, and I do not smooth over a name they spell two ways.

Early (Ch 1 to 2): rob Illegal Operations (Key Lento's Exotic Animals Front behind Three Sheets Liquor; Kingfisher Fishing Shacks and Manatee Hotel in the swamps) and sell narcotics to Brian or Donnie (Ch 1) or Dunk (Ch 2); sell cars; treasure-dive for Kenton.

Note from the fact file: VICE spells the Ch2 buyer 'Dunk' and also 'Bunk' in the same article.

  • VICE (19 November 2026)

Stealing cars: Moses (Ch 2) buys older, low-profile cars, ~$1,000 to a few thousand each; Randy (Ch 3) pays more for high-end cars. Range quoted $1,000 to $20,000.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)

Hunting for Bret (Killtelligence, Lummox County, Jason only, Ch 2): PC Gamer was paid $2,500 for a large boar; Polygon says up to a few thousand per pristine skin; Bret arranges pickup for large animals.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Don't worry too much about money early: the opening chapters can take 20+ hours and money gets easier later.

  • The Guardian (Keza MacDonald) (19 November 2026)

Ranges the guides publish later

These are the ranges and examples in the fact file. I have not turned them into an hourly rate.

Demolition Derby at Ambrosia Raceway: $3,800 to $6,300 per win, repeatable.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)

Back-door store robberies after the Ch 3 snap gun: $6,000 to $15,000 (PC Gamer). Jewellery stores: take value roughly $20,000 (Polygon), e.g. Victoria Lee Jeweler (Capri), Blanc and Giroux (Decor District).

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Pilot Pete's drops (meet in Ch 3, work in Ch 4): $10,000 to $20,000 per run.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)

Selling a car at the Ocean Beach Pay 'N' Spray: roughly $7,000 for a valuable car (Polygon). Bank stick-up (cash registers only, late Ch 3): about $15,000 per robbery.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Collectibles named by Polygon: cards, doubloons, meteorites (sell at pawn shops for a few hundred each). GameSpot's cheat list references collectible watches and 24 Vice City Flamingo cards.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • GameSpot (18 November 2026)

Where the sale limit is not settled

I am leaving the Pay 'n' Spray sale limit as a disagreement.

Pay 'N' Spray car-sale limits disagree: PC Gamer says three cars per day; VICE says a limit per 24 in-game hours (and Moses has no daily limit); Polygon says a brief cooldown.

Sources differ on this, so I am showing the disagreement instead of picking a winner.

  • PC Gamer (18 November 2026)
  • VICE (19 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
