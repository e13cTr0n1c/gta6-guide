Can you buy a house in GTA 6?

No. I cannot buy my own home. The Vice City Real Estate app only sells garages, and safehouses come with the story.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

What the guides actually say

You can't buy your own home. The Vice City Real Estate app sells garages only (Polygon). Safehouses come with the story.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
  • VICE (17 November 2026)

What I can buy instead

Garages are the property in the reputable coverage. The prices are on the garage page.

Houses/businesses: you can't buy your own home (Polygon). No purchasable businesses found in reputable coverage. Garages are the only buyable property.

Vice City Real Estate unlocks after Moses' first quest in Ch 2, when you steal the '82 Crawler for him.

Note from the fact file: Non-list sources say after mission 'Daily Driver' (Ch 2, mission 6), which may be the same moment.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Garage prices and locations: see prices table (10 garages per GameSpot).
