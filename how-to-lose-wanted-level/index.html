How to lose your wanted level (heat) in GTA 6

First I break line of sight and get out of the red heat zone on the map. Then I clear each heat icon: I change my outfit or put on a hat and sunglasses, swap or respray the car, stow longarms, split from my partner, and destroy CCTV.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

Stars and the red zone

Stars show wanted level (up to six). 1 star ~ one police car, 2 stars several cars, 3 stars helicopter. Escape the red heat zone to clear stars.

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

Clearing each heat icon

Heat icons and fixes: appearance (cap/sunglasses/haircut), outfit (change clothes), vehicle (respray at Pay 'N' Spray or swap), visible weapon (holster or stow longarm in trunk), partner (split up), CCTV (destroy recorder; cloud CCTV can't be destroyed).

  • Polygon (19 November 2026)

How many stars a crime is worth

I only have one source for those star counts, and another guide is looser, so I treat the numbers as approximate.

Guardian: stealing a car is one star, robbing a store three or more, killing cops always adds a star.

I only have this from one source, so I am treating it as thinner than the rest.
