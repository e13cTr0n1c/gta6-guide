The cash limits

Pocket cash: each character can carry up to $20,000. All pocket cash is lost if Wasted or Busted. Cash covers purchases up to $1,000; larger purchases need BuckMe.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)

BuckMe checking account unlocks in Chapter 1 (mission 'Buck Me'); each character has their own; balance limits rise with story progress (upgrades in Ch 2, 3 and 4). Over the limit, incoming payments are held as pending.

  • GamesRadar+ (19 November 2026)
  • Polygon (19 November 2026)
