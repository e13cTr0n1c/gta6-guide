Two readings of capacity

PC Gamer says each garage holds a single car. I have not ticked a list garage, so that reading adds 0 slots. With the 4 starting slots, that reading is 4. I am not folding Valentina's garage into this number, because GameSpot gives that garage its own slot count.

GameSpot says the list garages hold one or a couple of vehicles, so I will not turn those 0 garages into one slot total. GameSpot's exact figures on this form are the 4 starting slots. Valentina's Ocean Beach garage is 2 slots in GameSpot's account, and I have not ticked it. GameSpot also says 12 or more slots are possible. That line is theirs, not a total I calculated from the ticks.
