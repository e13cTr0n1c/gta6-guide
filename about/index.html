What is this GTA 6 fan guide?

I wrote this as an unofficial fan guide for people starting GTA 6. I am not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

How a fact gets onto a page

Every game fact on the question pages is copied from the fact file in this project. I keep the outlet and the link with the claim.

The fact file limits me to these outlets: Reputable outlets only: official Rockstar/PlayStation, IGN, PC Gamer, GameSpot, Polygon, GamesRadar+, Eurogamer, The Verge, The Guardian, VICE. Fan wikis excluded.

Where the file says the sources differ, or that something is unknown, reported, or speculation, I say that in plain words. I do not pick a winner, and I do not fill a gap from a fan wiki.

I do not use official artwork, official typefaces, or official marks. The shapes at the top of the page are mine. There are no ads and no trackers.

The date on each page is the day I last checked the fact file: 20 November 2026.
