How big is GTA 6? File size and storage

Rockstar says up to 212GB depending on the device, and the PlayStation page says to clear at least 212GB. Preload opened on 10 November. I would clear 212GB, because the fact file says that is the safe reading when the wording differs.

Last checked: 20 November 2026

The two wordings

The wording differs, so I am not pretending the two pages say the same thing.

Preload/install size is 'up to 212GB' depending on device (Rockstar). PlayStation US page says clear 'a minimum of 212GB'.

Note from the fact file: Wording differs: Rockstar 'up to 212GB' vs PlayStation 'minimum of 212GB'. Safe advice: clear 212GB.

Preload, and which consoles

Preload began 10 November 2026: PS5 at midnight local time; Xbox Series X|S at 00:00 GMT worldwide.
